## A. Summary

**What we found:** the South has no visible GS5 importer or stockist.

- We ran about 560 web searches in 15 research batches, over three rounds: discovery, gap-filling and number verification.
- No supply-side seller in the five states shows "GS5" or "Game Station 5" in a listing title or photo.
- Every GS5 or GS5 Pro wholesale listing we found is in Delhi or Ghaziabad (Rs500–4,500), or in Surat. The same goes for the importers named on Amazon and Flipkart (Delhi, Haryana, Nagpur).
- The Southern supply sits one level down. Video-game traders in Chennai, Hyderabad and Vijayawada list "8-bit / 16-bit TV video game consoles" and "620-game mini game boxes" on IndiaMART, and Kerala and Tamil Nadu toy importers and wholesalers carry imported electronic toys.
- These traders almost certainly restock from Delhi's Gaffar Market and Lajpat Rai Market. On the phone, ask them who they buy from. That question will name the distributor.

### What was blocked

- **Downloads blocked:** this cloud environment's network policy blocks downloads from youtube.com, IndiaMART, JustDial, Amazon, Flipkart, Google Maps, magicpin, TradeIndia, bharatdirectory, Instagram and most firms' own websites. vidIQ had no credits.
  - The research therefore ran entirely through web search. Its extended mode reads those pages behind the scenes and quotes them, but it can't open a listing, a Maps card or a YouTube description directly.
  - Search results sometimes combine several pages into one answer. Any number we couldn't trace to one URL is marked "unattributed" and graded C at most.
- **Search cap:** web search allows 200 searches per turn across all researchers, which cut round 1 short. Rounds 2 and 3 filled the gaps.
- **IndiaMART relay numbers:** most IndiaMART sellers in the South show only a relay number (29 rejected numbers, see C), so many FAMILY sellers have no real number.
- **Searched, nothing usable:** searches in Tamil, Telugu and Malayalam script, Moazzam Jahi Market (a produce market), Warangal, Kurnool, Kakinada, Kottayam, Kannur, Erode, Vellore, Hosur and Tiruppur.
- **How to fix it:** to let a re-run open these pages directly, widen Network access in this environment's settings (cloud environment menu → Edit → Network access; https://code.claude.com/docs/en/cloud-environments#network-access). That would allow opening YouTube descriptions, Amazon importer fields and Maps listings, which would likely lift several grade-C numbers to A.

## D. "Ask on the call" checklist

1. **GS5 or GS5 Pro?** GS5 is the 8-bit vertical box: 200–620 games, 2 wired pads, AV/HDMI. GS5 Pro is 64-bit HDMI with 10,000–15,000 games and retails at Rs2,500–3,900. Make sure they don't mean the "G5" handheld (Rs300–450).
2. **Real game count:** ask how many *different* games. A menu that says "4,000" or "30,000" usually repeats 200–400 titles.
3. **AV or HDMI output?**
4. **Wholesale price at 10, 50 and 100 pieces.** Benchmarks: Rs520 in Delhi, Rs700 in Surat, Rs850 in Bengaluru; China FOB is US$5.1–7.5.
5. **Minimum order?**
6. **GST bill available?** Take their GSTIN and check it before paying.
7. **Dead-on-arrival replacement or warranty:** how long is the window, and who pays the courier?
8. **Who do they buy from?** This names the distributor. Record it in the sheet.
9. Is the stock in the shop, or ordered from Delhi or China? What is the lead time and courier cost to Bengaluru?
10. Ask for a WhatsApp photo of the actual box showing "GS5" / "Game Station 5".

## How this was built

- **Round 1 (discovery):** six parallel researchers covered Chennai, the rest of Tamil Nadu plus Puducherry, Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, Kerala, and a product-first sweep of marketplaces and IndiaMART.
- **Round 2 (gaps):** five researchers searched the cities that hadn't been covered, ran a dedicated EXACT-GS5 hunt, and hunted for second sources.
- **Round 3 (numbers):** four researchers checked every grade-C number by searching the number itself and the shop name, and followed up the GS5 leads.
- **Merging:** duplicates were removed by phone number and by name plus city. A later round's correction always wins, even when it lowers a grade. Relay numbers are caught automatically (0804x, 0794x, 07961/65/71, 8071).
- **Search log:** every query is in the "Searches run" sheet of the xlsx.
